## Mission

The Naso Software Foundation is an independent, community-driven organization that stewards the Naso programming language, its ecosystem, and its standards. Our mission is to ensure Naso remains:

- **Open**: Free for everyone to use, modify, and distribute
- **Neutral**: No single vendor controls the language direction
- **Sustainable**: Long-term funding for core development
- **Accessible**: Low barriers to entry for contributors and users

## Governance Structure

### Board of Directors

The Board is the highest governing body, responsible for:

- Setting strategic direction
- Approving the annual budget
- Electing/removing Technical Steering Committee members
- Trademark and IP management
- Legal and financial oversight

**Board Composition** (7 members):
- 3 elected by individual contributors
- 2 elected by corporate members
- 1 appointed by the Technical Steering Committee
- 1 community representative (rotating annually)

**Term**: 3 years, staggered elections, maximum 2 consecutive terms

### Technical Steering Committee (TSC)

The TSC manages technical direction:

- Approves/rejects language proposals (NEP process)
- Manages release schedules
- Oversees compiler and stdlib development
- Maintains the language specification
- Resolves technical disputes

**TSC Composition** (9 members):
- 5 elected by contributors with 10+ merged PRs
- 2 appointed by corporate members (Gold+)
- 1 appointed by the Board
- 1 BDFL (Benevolent Dictator For Life) - the language creator

**Term**: 2 years, staggered elections

### Working Groups

Working groups focus on specific domains:

| Group | Focus | Lead |
|-------|-------|------|
| Language | Core language evolution | TSC chair |
| Stdlib | Standard library | Stdlib maintainer |
| Compiler | Compiler backends | Compiler lead |
| Quantum | Quantum computing | Quantum lead |
| Verification | Formal verification | Verification lead |
| Registry | Package registry | Registry maintainer |
| Docs | Documentation | Docs lead |
| Community | Community growth | Community manager |
| Security | Security and vulnerabilities | Security lead |

### Community Council

The Community Council represents individual contributors:

- Organizes contributor events
- Manages mentorship program
- Runs contributor recognition
- Coordinates outreach and diversity initiatives
- Advises the Board on community needs

## Decision-Making Process

### NEP (Naso Enhancement Proposal)

All significant changes go through the NEP process:

1. **Draft**: Author writes NEP, posts to discussion forum
2. **Review**: Community provides feedback (minimum 2 weeks)
3. **TSC Vote**: TSC votes (simple majority required)
4. **Implement**: Author implements, TSC reviews
5. **Release**: Feature included in next edition

**Voting thresholds**:
- Language changes: 2/3 TSC majority
- Stdlib changes: Simple TSC majority
- Process changes: Simple TSC majority
- Emergency fixes: TSC chair + 1 member

### Contributor Tiers

| Tier | Contribution | Voting Rights | Benefits |
|------|-------------|---------------|----------|
| Contributor | 1+ merged PR | NEP discussion | Credit, swag |
| Member | 10+ merged PRs | TSC election vote | Voting, early access |
| Maintainer | 50+ merged PRs, 6+ months | Working group lead | Direct repo access |
| Emeritus | Retired after 2+ years | Lifetime voting | Lifetime recognition |

## Intellectual Property

### Licensing

- Naso compiler and stdlib: MIT OR Apache-2.0
- Documentation: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Trademark "Naso": Owned by the Foundation
- Patent pledge: Contributors grant royalty-free patent licenses

### Trademark Policy

- "Naso" and the Naso logo are Foundation trademarks
- Use in derivative works requires attribution
- Commercial use of trademark requires license
- Community use (meetups, blogs) is free with guidelines

### Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

All contributors must sign the Naso CLA:

- Contributor retains copyright
- Contributor grants Foundation non-exclusive license
- Foundation can relicense under compatible open source licenses
- No patent assertions against Naso users
